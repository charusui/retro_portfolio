Overview

Security that works before the scam does

Digital fraud reaches people through the channels they trust most: text messages, chat apps and phone calls. Seguri protects the users most likely to be targeted by spotting a threat before they act on it.

Objectives

  • Detect phishing links, scam messages and fraudulent calls in one app.
  • Keep personal messages private by analysing on the device first.
  • Warn users fast enough to matter, with a target under 200 ms.
01

The problem

Most scam protection forces a trade-off. Cloud-only detection is smart but means sending private conversations to a server. On-device-only detection keeps data private but can miss new, cleverly worded scams. Vulnerable users can't be expected to judge that trade-off themselves.

The design tension: how do you get cloud-level intelligence without giving up on-device privacy?

— Core question behind Seguri
02

The process

The work moved from understanding threats to proving the system was fast and accurate enough to trust.

  1. Step 1

    Map threats

    Group attacks by channel: links, messages and calls.

  2. Step 2

    Design alerts

    Clear, calm warnings with one obvious action.

  3. Step 3

    Build hybrid AI

    100% offline on-device scanning with optional Gemini cloud features.

  4. Step 4

    Measure

    Tune for speed (<200 ms) and accuracy (98%).

03

The solution

Seguri's Hybrid-AI Security Suite combines an offline-first on-device neural network with optional cloud intelligence. Key decisions:

  • 100% on-device core: 24/7 Active Guard and volatile RAM-only scanner run locally in milliseconds with zero data upload.
  • Context & heuristics: dual-layer TFLite model detects regional slang (e.g. Taglish scams), while Dart heuristics eliminate false alarms on safe family texts and OTPs.
  • Empathetic protection: high-contrast plain-language alerts, an offline step-by-step Scam First Aid Kit, and a secure local evidence log.
04

The result

A single app that screens every major scam channel quickly and accurately.

Takeaways

What was hard

Balancing speed and accuracy. Every call to the cloud adds latency, so deciding when to escalate mattered as much as the models themselves.

What I took away

Security for vulnerable users is a UX problem as much as an AI problem. The best detection is useless if the warning confuses people.
